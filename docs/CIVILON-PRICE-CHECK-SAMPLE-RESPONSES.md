## Safe wording substitutions

| Avoid | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| “You overpaid” / “You were ripped off” | “The submitted price was above the observed comparable range.” |
| “The supplier cheated you” | “The result does not assess supplier conduct.” |
| “Fair market value” / “appraised value” | “Observed comparable range/indications.” |
| “The supplier’s cost/margin is…” | Omit; Civilon does not disclose or infer it. |
| “The AI found the market price” | “Staff reviewed the transaction; the deterministic engine calculated the range from selected governed evidence.” |
| “This certifies the value” | “This informational Price Check is not an appraisal or valuation certificate.” |
