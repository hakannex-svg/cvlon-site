# Civilon content requiring owner confirmation

The following statements appeared on the previous reference site but are intentionally excluded from the new pages until an owner supplies an approved source:

- Exact AS9120 and ISO 9001 certification status, scope, certificate numbers, and expiry dates.
- Any claim that Civilon itself certifies, releases, or physically repairs aircraft parts.
- Specific quote-response promises such as “under one hour” or “within 30 minutes.”
- Guaranteed availability, same-day dispatch, 24/7 live-person response, hand-carry, next-flight-out, or specific international delivery methods.
- Exact proximity or travel time to EWR, JFK, or other airports.
- Any warehouse, inventory-volume, transaction-volume, employee-count, customer-count, or founding-date claims.

Approved cautious language retained: Civilon sources parts, reviews documentation, coordinates repair and logistics, and requests FAA 8130-3 or EASA Form 1 where applicable.
